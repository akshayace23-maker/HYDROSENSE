About

From citizen observation to water-body restoration

HydroSense is a citizen-centric digital platform designed to improve the monitoring and protection of local water bodies. It addresses the gap between what people observe and what gets documented, routed, and followed up by connecting citizen reports, photographic evidence, GPS location, community confirmation, and authority action into one continuous record.

How it works

Observe → Report → Locate → Confirm → Authority verifies → Inspection → Action → Follow-up evidence → Before/After → Long-term water-body history.

What HydroSense is not

HydroSense does not replace official environmental inspection or laboratory testing, and it does not claim that a photograph or an AI-assisted check can confirm water pollution, toxicity, or contamination on its own. It clearly separates four kinds of information, and never blends them:

What a citizen saw
Citizen Observation
What a photo may suggest
AI-Assisted Check
What a field visit confirms
Authority Inspection
What testing proves
Scientific Measurement
